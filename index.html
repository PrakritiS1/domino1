Our Story

Born from Passion

Italian Pizza was born in a small kitchen in 2012, when Chef Marco fell in love with Neapolitan pizza-making traditions. What started as a weekend passion became Patna's most beloved pizzeria. Every pizza is a tribute to the craft — 72-hour cold-fermented dough, San Marzano tomatoes, and buffalo mozzarella imported weekly.

Today we serve over 500 customers daily across three locations, but our kitchen still feels like home — because it is.
